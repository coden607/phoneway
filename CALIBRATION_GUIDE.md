# Phoneway Calibration Guide (for best accuracy)

## Setup
1. Place the phone on a **soft, stable surface** (mouse pad, thick notebook, or folded cloth). Hard tables reduce signal.
2. Phone must be **flat and still**. Avoid charging during measurement (heat drift).
3. Let the phone rest 1–2 minutes after moving it.

## Steps
1. **Power on** and wait for automatic tare (zero). Nothing should be on the phone.
2. **First calibration**: Place a known weight (US nickel ≈ 5.00 g is ideal). Wait until the display shows stable, then confirm calibration.
3. **Second point** (strongly recommended): Add another known weight (dollar bill ≈ 1 g, or a second coin). This improves the linear/quadratic fit.
4. **Verify**: Remove the weight, re-tare if needed, then re-weigh the same object. Compare to known value.
5. Re-calibrate if you change surfaces, temperature changes significantly, or accuracy drifts.

## Tips for ±0.2–0.5 g
- Use the softest practical surface.
- Keep the phone perfectly still during the stable window.
- Multi-point calibration (2–3 known weights) is far better than a single point.
- Extreme accuracy (±0.1 g) requires excellent conditions and is not guaranteed on all phones/sensors.

## Troubleshooting
- “Signal too weak”: Softer surface or heavier reference weight.
- Readings jump: Reduce motion, improve surface, re-tare.
- Persistent offset: Re-calibrate with two different known weights.
